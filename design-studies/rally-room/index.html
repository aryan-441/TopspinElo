Colour notes

Azure #3283BE and snowy white #FEFDFE stay fixed. Harbour slate #5C7C94 uses 40% of the blue’s original HSL saturation. Coral is the hand-picked accent; raspberry and citron turn the blue hue by +137.5° and −137.5° respectively, keeping its saturation and lightness.
