One connected record

A result can mean more than a score.

Record only the winner when that is all you know. Add game scores for more detail, or record each point to study service, receiving and pressure moments. TopspinElo uses the detail you actually enter, so a final score never becomes an invented point history.

Elo here is an internal TopspinElo rating, not an official federation ranking. Deeper statistics need deeper scoring records.

For clubs and regular players

Make ordinary club nights count.

Keep a player directory, record friendly matches, see current club ratings and revisit recent form. You do not need a formal tournament or a camera to get started. Coaches can use the numbers to spot questions worth exploring at training.

For organisers and officials

Keep the event moving.

Build groups and knockout draws, choose any match ready to play, assign referees when needed and review every result. Organisers can correct draws and schedules, see change history and export their data. PDF bracket import offers a review step before applying a recognised draw.

Referee accounts and shared permissions are part of the planned online release. This preview uses separate organiser and referee views on one device.

For players and coaches

See the match beyond the win.

Look at match and game records, current Elo, doubles partnerships and the point statistics supported by your scoring history. Serve, return and deuce rates need recorded points. Those numbers can guide a conversation with a coach; they do not diagnose technique on their own.

Built from the table up

TopspinElo began in late 2025 as a Python project for matches and tournaments. The original Tkinter desktop plan grew into a web app connecting competition operations and player development.
